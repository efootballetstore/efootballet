eFootball Accounts in Ethiopia: Buy, Sell or Exchange

On eFootballET you can buy an eFootball account from a seller, sell your own account, or send an exchange offer. You talk to the other person in the chat on the site, and eFootballET checks the account before any payment is unlocked.

Buy an account

Browse the accounts list, send a buy request, and when the seller accepts you pay within the time shown. The first buyer to pay gets the account.

አካውንቶችን ይመልከቱ፣ የግዢ ጥያቄ ይላኩ፣ ሻጩ ሲቀበል በሚታየው ጊዜ ውስጥ ይክፈሉ። መጀመሪያ የከፈለው አካውንቱን ያገኛል።

Sell your account

Open Sell your account, fill in the details and send. Our team reviews it before it is listed. Your login details go only to eFootballET staff.

"አካውንትዎን ይሽጡ" ይክፈቱ፣ ዝርዝሩን ሞልተው ይላኩ። ከመዘርዘሩ በፊት ቡድናችን ይገመግመዋል። የመግቢያ መረጃዎ ለeFootballET ሠራተኞች ብቻ ይደርሳል።

Exchange accounts

Send an exchange offer in the chat. When the seller accepts, eFootballET verifies both accounts before payment is unlocked.
